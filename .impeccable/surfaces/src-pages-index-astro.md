# Surface brief: home (single page)

Mode: Read, with the restraint of a reading page (ROADMAP.md → Design brief: "persuade, with the restraint of a reading page"). Code-led: no image generation, and a Read surface builds code-led.

Audience and job: parents, colleagues and contracting parties checking who she is; they leave trusting her registration, training and conduct. Proof is checkable fact (CRM, courses, workplaces), never claims. Constraints: CFM checklist, no client JavaScript, no third-party request, tokens from DESIGN.md only.

Issue 2.1 scope: the brand under the whole page (tokens, type roles, header, links, focus, section grounds, footer, social image). Sections are finished one by one in 2.2 to 2.4.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The registration card, in her colours. The page reads as a well-set document whose warmth comes from her brand fields, refusing the clinic-template hero, the card grid and the stock-photo medical look.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; light fields only, white and verde-claro alternating by section, Sobre on lilás-claro with a 4px lilás rule; no bands (Filipe, 2026-11-16). Ink is azul-tinta; headings and links verde-profundo; ouro only as a 1–2px hairline; footer a verde-profundo field with the white logo. Source Serif 4 600 for name and headings, Source Sans 3 for reading and the tracked capitals identity line. Square corners, flat, no shadows, no icons.

STORY: The visitor sees her logo as a signature, then her name, MÉDICA and CRM-SP 240550 in live text, then the evidence in order, and closes on a deep green footer that repeats who she is.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a phone, a white header with the small colour horizontal logo and the section links in label capitals, then the name in display serif, the identity line in tracked capitals, the 4:5 portrait. On a wide screen the portrait sits beside the name, never behind it.

FORM: Brand pinned whole by the client and recorded in DESIGN.md; page sequence pinned by the brief. seed: none (pinned) — "Manter paleta no verde, lilás/roxo, azul, amarelo, mas podemos mudar as fontes das letras para fontes mais sóbrias"; and the confirmed design brief (ROADMAP.md): "identification with portrait → Formação → Atuação → Sobre → Contato", "First fold: name, MÉDICA, CRM-SP 240550 and the professional photo, all in real text".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
